Sources &

What we used, what each source tells us, and where the evidence stops. Updated October 22, 2026.

MySchools

School and program descriptions, eligibility, admissions methods, priorities, GE/SWD seats, demand and prior-year offers. Signed-in checks also verified application-specific attributes and high-school offer estimates.

Historical demand is not a current acceptance probability. The public directory and account-specific estimates are different sources; private account links and identifiers are excluded from this register.

MySchools ↗

NYCPS admissions rules

Official explanations of district and zone priority, random numbers, screening, specialized admissions and offer chances. We use these to interpret program data rather than treating raw demand ratios as individual odds.

A future neighborhood does not automatically change an applicant’s verified priority. Individual placement must be confirmed.

InsideSchools

Independent narrative reviews, classroom observations, strengths and concerns, school profiles, and the “Staff Pick for Special Ed” search filter. Review dates are shown separately from statistics.

Some narratives are older or cover a combined middle/high school. A staff pick is an editorial endorsement, not a guarantee of a particular classroom, support model or admission.

School Quality Snapshot

The official 2024–25 elementary/middle-school report supplies attendance, academic indicators, high-school credit and special-education service-delivery figures. Report links and selected values are shown for each public middle school.

Report year and individual measurement periods are not interchangeable. K–8 figures may combine grades; population-level service delivery does not establish an individual placement.

Understand the Snapshot ↗

2026 NYC School Survey

Official family, student and teacher public spreadsheets supply composite scores, respondent counts and response rates. The guide separates the 2026 survey from the older Snapshot and InsideSchools statistics.

Scores are out of 100, not individual-question percentages. The redesigned student survey establishes a new baseline; do not read it as a prior-year trend. Responses may cover multiple grades.

School websites & visits

Official school pages document ICT, literacy and executive-function support, curriculum, athletics, locations, tours, open houses and private-school admission procedures.

Published provision is not confirmation of an individual timetable. Dates, staffing, sports availability and grade-specific services need checking with the school.

Exact school-specific links are indexed below.

Videos & virtual tours

Verified YouTube links, official video galleries, Vimeo tours and selected student-life features. Watch lists label publishers, dates and whether a recording is unofficial, old, program-specific or combined-school footage.

Search links are labeled as searches, not verified videos. Promotional videos are useful for visit preparation, not independent evidence of school quality.

Maps & location evidence

MySchools building coordinates, official directions and campus maps, NYC Planning GeoSearch address matches and NYC DCP LION streets inform locations and nearby intersections. Google Maps links support individual journeys; Leaflet and OpenStreetMap display the shortlist maps.

Map pins indicate buildings, not a guaranteed visitor entrance. Commute descriptions are planning judgments, not live travel-time measurements.

Family context & judgments

Family-provided context and shared comments inform recommendations and tour questions. These remain separate from verified institutional facts. Unverified concerns are identified as family feedback.

Rankings are editorial tour priorities, not official ratings or admission predictions. Private profiles, notes and account data are excluded from this public bibliography.
