One shared Consortium essay

East Side Community, ICE and School of the Future use the same essay. Answer one of the two official prompts in 500 words or fewer, then submit through MySchools → Additional Materials. Choose either a response about how this learning approach would help you grow and what you would contribute, or a reflection on an intellectual challenge, inspiration or risk and its impact. Complete it once, and rank each desired school separately. East Side and ICE use the essay for 100% of their score; School of the Future uses it for 30%.

Beacon has its own in-person written response. A shared essay upload does not complete Beacon’s assessment.
